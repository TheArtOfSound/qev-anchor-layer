# Disclaimer

QAL (QEV Anchor Layer) is open-source software provided **as is**, without
warranty of any kind.

## Not legal advice / not notarization

Anchoring a digest on Solana is **not**:

- A notarial act
- Proof of real-world identity or authorship
- Evidence of document authenticity under any particular jurisdiction
- A substitute for legal process

A wallet signature proves control of that wallet (subject to key security),
nothing more.

## Not a security guarantee

- No claim of being unhackable, military-grade, or quantum-proof
- Verified program builds do not imply a professional security audit
- Bugs may exist in QAL, QEV, Solana, wallets, or dependencies

## Data permanence

Public ledger data and optional IPFS ciphertext may be **permanently
retrievable**. Do not publish encrypted material you are not prepared to have
stored forever.

## Loss of secrets

If the QEV passphrase is lost, QAL cannot recover plaintext. Revoking an
on-chain endorsement does **not** erase plaintext already decrypted by any party.

## No hosted custody

QAL v0.1 does not require Qira accounts and does not custody plaintext or keys.
