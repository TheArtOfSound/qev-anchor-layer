## Hook (0:00–0:03)

**On screen:** Dark 3D theater + big **Play the idea →** on the lock/chain/check stage.  
**Voice / caption:**

**Text overlay:** `🔒 Lock offline`

## Act 2 — Fingerprint (0:06–0:09)

**On screen:** **Stamp fingerprint** → hash types out → pick “Just the fingerprint + status.”
